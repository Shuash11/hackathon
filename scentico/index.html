Our Story

Built like a good cup
of coffee — slow, warm,
impossible to fake.

Scentico started in a small studio where we treated fragrance the way a roaster treats beans: testing, discarding, and testing again until a scent felt inevitable. Every bottle still goes through that same patience today.

  • Small batches, blended and bottled by hand in-house.
  • Sourced resins, woods and florals — no shortcuts, no fillers.
  • Designed to soften and deepen over the hours you wear it.
